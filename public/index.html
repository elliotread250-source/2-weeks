TWO WEEKS

Build. Edit. Drop on Rebirth Island. Be the last one standing.

Controls

The drop

Jump off the Battle Bus, skydive, and your glider pops automatically near the ground.

You land with a pickaxe, 100 health and 100 shield. Farm wood from trees, brick from rocks and metal from cars and containers. Hit the blue weak point for double materials.

Loot runs Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary. Chests glow gold.

Resurgence: like Rebirth Island, you redeploy after dying until the third storm circle starts. After that, it's for keeps.
